Parse the values that follow a directive keyword

floats(in:_:) parses every token on the line, so "DOMAIN_MIN 0.0 0.0 0.0"
failed on the keyword itself and every table carrying a DOMAIN declaration was
rejected with "malformedNumber(DOMAIN_MIN)". values(after:in:_:) drops the keyword
first.

Also two test corrections:
- The error-message test asserted every message names a line, but .empty, .notUTF8
  and a whole-file sample-count mismatch genuinely have none. Those three are now
  asserted to claim no line, and everything else is asserted to have one.
- A test used try! on a parse, which turned an ordinary assertion failure into a
  process crash and buried the real output.
