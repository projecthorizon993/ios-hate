Detect .cube data lines without throwing

floats(in:_:) needs a line number and throws, but meeting a directive line is
expected rather than an error, so using it as the data-line test threw away the
line number and made the call site wrong. allNumbers(in:) answers the question
directly and floats(in:_:) keeps its line number for the two DOMAIN directives,
where a non-number really is a malformed file.
